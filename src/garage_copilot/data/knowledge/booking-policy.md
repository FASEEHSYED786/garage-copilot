# Booking policy

## Opening hours and drop-off

The workshop is open Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm, and Saturday 8am to 1pm. Cars should be dropped off by 9am for same-day work.

## Courtesy cars

Courtesy cars are free for customers booked in for a service, MOT or repair that takes longer than four hours. They are allocated in booking order and must be reserved at the time of booking.

## Cancellations

Bookings can be cancelled or moved free of charge up to 24 hours before the appointment. Later cancellations of fitting work carry a £20 fee because parts are ordered in advance.

## Reviews

After each completed job the customer receives a review request by email and text message. We only send one reminder, three days later.
