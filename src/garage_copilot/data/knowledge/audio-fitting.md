# Car audio fitting notes

## Cars with a factory premium audio system

Cars fitted with a factory premium system (for example Dynaudio, Harman Kardon, Bang & Olufsen, Bose or JBL) use an external factory amplifier. Replacing the head unit needs an amplifier interface that keeps the factory amp working, otherwise there will be no sound from the speakers.

Always confirm the audio system before quoting. The customer should choose their system on the booking page, and the fitter must check the amplifier location on arrival.

## Volkswagen Golf

A Golf with the Dynaudio premium system needs the premium amplifier interface and a CAN-bus adapter for steering wheel controls. A standard Golf without the premium system needs only the CAN-bus adapter.

Allow 2.5 hours for a head unit fit on a Golf with Dynaudio and 1.5 hours without.

## Steering wheel controls and parking sensors

Steering wheel controls need a CAN-bus interface on almost every car made after 2010. Cars with factory parking sensor displays on the screen need a visual parking sensor retention module, or the display will be lost.

## Reversing cameras

Aftermarket reversing cameras are wired to the reverse light for power. On cars with LED rear lights a relay or CAN-bus trigger is needed to avoid flicker.
