# MOT and servicing guide

## When an MOT is due

In England, Scotland and Wales a car needs its first MOT when it is three years old, then once every year. You can get an MOT up to a month (minus a day) before the current certificate runs out and keep the same renewal date.

Always check the official GOV.UK MOT history service for the exact expiry date before booking. Our system calculates the due date as one year after the most recent pass.

## Advisories and failures

An advisory is something the tester noticed that is not bad enough to fail, such as brake pads wearing thin or a slight oil leak. Advisories are the best early warning we have: a car with two or more advisories on its last test is much more likely to fail the next one.

A major or dangerous defect is a fail. A car with a dangerous defect must not be driven away until it is repaired.

## Our service intervals

Our standard interval is every 10,000 miles or 12 months, whichever comes first. High-mileage drivers covering more than 15,000 miles a year should book an interim service at 6 months.

Electric vehicles follow a 2-year or 20,000-mile schedule focused on brakes, tyres, suspension, cabin filter and coolant for the battery system.

## Pre-MOT check

We offer a 20-minute pre-MOT check for cars at high risk of failure. It covers lights, tyres, brakes, wipers, screen damage and emissions warning lights. Recommend it when the MOT risk score is high or the last test had advisories on brakes or tyres.
