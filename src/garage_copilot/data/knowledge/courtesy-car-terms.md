# Courtesy car terms

## Who can drive

The hirer must hold a full UK driving licence for at least one year. We check the licence at collection and take a photo of it. Additional drivers must be added at collection and have their licence checked too.

## Collection and return

At collection we record the mileage, fuel level and photos of all four sides of the car, and the hirer signs on screen. A signed copy is emailed to the hirer straight away.

The car must be returned by 6pm on the agreed day with the same fuel level. Missing fuel is charged at the pump price plus a £10 refuelling fee.

## Damage and excess

The hirer is responsible for damage during the hire, up to an excess of £250. We compare the collection and return photos to agree any new damage before the hirer leaves.

## Fines and penalty charges

Parking fines, speeding notices and congestion or clean air zone charges are the hirer's responsibility. We look up who had the car by registration and date, and transfer the notice to them with a copy of the signed terms. An admin fee of £25 applies to each notice.

## Late returns

Late returns are charged at £30 per extra day. If a car is more than 24 hours overdue and we cannot reach the hirer, we may report it to the police.
